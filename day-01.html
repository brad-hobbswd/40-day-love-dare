The 40 Day Journey · Day 1 of 40

Choose Love Intentionally

Scripture for reflection: 1 Corinthians 13:4–7

Today's Coaching

Love becomes visible when we choose actions that communicate care. Today, focus on what you can give rather than keeping score of what you receive. A small, sincere act can help your spouse feel remembered and valued.

Put love into action

Your practice today

Choose one thoughtful action your spouse would genuinely appreciate. Do it freely, without demanding praise or an immediate return.

Personal Reflection

What helps my spouse feel loved in ordinary life? What is one habit I can practice more consistently?

A Prayer for Today

Jesus, teach me to love with sincerity and intention. Help my actions communicate care, patience, and respect. Amen.

This challenge is an invitation to practice care, not a test of worth or a substitute for professional support. Never use a challenge to pressure, control, or endanger a spouse.
