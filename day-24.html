The 40 Day Journey · Day 24 of 40

Support Each Other’s Calling

Scripture for reflection: Romans 12:10

Today's Coaching

Marriage should not erase each person’s gifts, responsibilities, or purpose. Support includes asking thoughtful questions, celebrating meaningful efforts, and discussing how individual goals fit alongside shared responsibilities.

Put love into action

Your practice today

Ask your spouse about one goal that matters deeply to them and what support would be helpful this week.

Personal Reflection

How can we encourage each other’s growth while caring for shared responsibilities?

A Prayer for Today

God, help us honor each other’s gifts and support one another with love. Amen.

This challenge is an invitation to practice care, not a test of worth or a substitute for professional support. Never use a challenge to pressure, control, or endanger a spouse.
