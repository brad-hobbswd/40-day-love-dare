The 40 Day Journey · Day 16 of 40

Honor Different Needs

Scripture for reflection: Romans 12:10

Today's Coaching

Two people can value the same relationship while needing different kinds of rest, conversation, affection, or support. Respect begins with asking and learning rather than insisting that one preference fits both people.

Put love into action

Your practice today

Ask your spouse what helps them feel supported this week, then share your own need without blame.

Personal Reflection

Where do our preferences differ, and how can we honor both?

A Prayer for Today

Lord, teach us to honor one another while communicating our needs honestly. Amen.

This challenge is an invitation to practice care, not a test of worth or a substitute for professional support. Never use a challenge to pressure, control, or endanger a spouse.
