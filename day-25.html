The 40 Day Journey · Day 25 of 40

Share the Load

Scripture for reflection: Ecclesiastes 4:9

Today's Coaching

Partnership works best when responsibilities are discussed rather than silently assumed. Fairness means both people can speak honestly about capacity and needs.

Put love into action

Your practice today

Review one shared responsibility together and discuss whether the current arrangement works for both of you.

Personal Reflection

Which invisible tasks or pressures need a more honest conversation?

A Prayer for Today

Lord, help us work as partners and notice the burdens each of us carries. Amen.

This challenge is an invitation to practice care, not a test of worth or a substitute for professional support. Never use a challenge to pressure, control, or endanger a spouse.
