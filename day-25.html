The 40 Day Journey · Day 25 of 40

Create a Ritual of Connection

Scripture for reflection: Song of Solomon 2:10

Today's Coaching

A simple, dependable ritual creates space for closeness amid busy routines. It might be a morning greeting, an evening check in, a short prayer, or a weekly walk. Consistency matters more than complexity.

Put love into action

Your practice today

Choose one small ritual you can realistically practice together and agree when to begin.

Personal Reflection

What regular moment could help us reconnect and stay aware of each other’s lives?

A Prayer for Today

Lord, help us protect meaningful time together and build habits that nurture closeness. Amen.

This challenge is an invitation to practice care, not a test of worth or a substitute for professional support. Never use a challenge to pressure, control, or endanger a spouse.
