About Coach Brad Hobbs

40 Day Love Dare with Your Spouse is an independent Christian devotional and coaching project created to encourage couples to put love into practice through everyday choices. Its focus is on biblical reflection, personal responsibility, respectful communication, forgiveness, and the steady work of building a caring relationship.

The aim is not to promise a perfect marriage or to place the burden of change on one spouse. It is to invite each person to consider the choices they can make, the ways they can communicate more thoughtfully, and the support they may need to grow.

The project is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the creators or publishers of the original The Love Dare book. Its original coaching reflections are developed separately.
