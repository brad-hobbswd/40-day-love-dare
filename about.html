The heart behind the project

About Coach Brad Hobbs

My journey into marriage coaching began with a personal experience that changed the way I understood love, commitment, forgiveness, and personal responsibility. The movie Fireproof and the message behind The Love Dare challenged me to look beyond what I wanted to receive from my marriage and consider what I could change within myself.

I did not approach that journey as someone who had everything figured out. I approached it as someone who was hurting, trying, learning, and hoping for restoration. I wanted my marriage to work, and I was willing to examine my own heart, seek counseling, and take steps toward personal growth. I understood that if change was going to begin somewhere, I had to be willing to begin with myself.

During that season, I was balancing work, church, family responsibilities, and the demands of raising a child while navigating the pain and uncertainty within my marriage. I learned that trying to hold everything together can leave a person emotionally exhausted, particularly when the desire to repair a relationship is accompanied by feelings of rejection, accusation, and disappointment.

My experience taught me something important. Personal growth is valuable, and intentional love matters, but one person cannot make every decision, carry every responsibility, or do the work required of two people. A healthy marriage requires honesty, mutual effort, accountability, and a willingness to grow together.

That is why I created this coaching initiative. I want to encourage couples to rediscover the importance of everyday choices, meaningful communication, forgiveness, and the kind of love that is demonstrated through action. I also want to remind individuals that working on themselves is worthwhile, even when the outcome of a relationship is not what they hoped it would be.

This challenge is not about pretending that marriage is easy or promising that forty days will repair every broken relationship. It is about inviting people to take an honest look at their own hearts, put love into practice, and discover what it means to pursue a relationship with intentionality and grace.

I know what it means to try. I know what it means to hope. And I know that even when a relationship does not unfold the way we prayed it would, the lessons we learn about ourselves, our faith, and our capacity to love can still have purpose.

My desire is to help others approach their relationships with greater understanding, humility, and intentionality, while remembering that love should never require a person to abandon their own well-being or carry the entire burden of a relationship alone.

This is more than a forty day challenge. It is an invitation to grow, to love with intention, and to take responsibility for the person we bring into our relationships.

The 40 Day Love Dare with Your Spouse is an independent Christian devotional and coaching project. Although Fireproof and The Love Dare helped inspire my journey, this project is independently developed and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the filmmakers, authors, or publishers of the original work.
