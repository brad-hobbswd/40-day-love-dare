REMINDER

1 Corinthians 13:4–7

Love Shows Up in the Little Things

Love is not demonstrated only through grand gestures or special occasions. It is also revealed through patience when a conversation takes longer than expected, kindness when stress is high, and a willingness to understand before responding. Today, look for one ordinary moment in which you can make your spouse feel seen, heard, and valued.

A simple practice for today

Ask your spouse, “What is one thing I could do today that would help you feel supported?” Listen without interrupting, and follow through if you are able.
