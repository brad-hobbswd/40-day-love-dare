The 40 Day Journey · Day 38 of 40

Renew Your Commitment

Scripture for reflection: Ruth 1:16–17

Today's Coaching

Commitment is lived through choices made repeatedly. It is not a reason to tolerate harm or abandon safety. In a healthy relationship, commitment is supported by respect, accountability, and care.

Put love into action

Your practice today

Talk about one commitment you want to practice more intentionally in the next month and identify one realistic action.

Personal Reflection

What does faithful commitment look like in our present season?

A Prayer for Today

Lord, help us practice commitment through honest, respectful, and loving choices. Amen.

This challenge is an invitation to practice care, not a test of worth or a substitute for professional support. Never use a challenge to pressure, control, or endanger a spouse.
