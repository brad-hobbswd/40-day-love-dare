The 40 Day Journey · Day 5 of 40

Notice the Little Things

Scripture for reflection: Song of Solomon 2:15

Today's Coaching

Relationships are shaped by small moments that can easily go unnoticed. Paying attention to the little things shows your spouse that their needs, efforts, and presence matter to you.

Put love into action

Your practice today

Notice one task, responsibility, or effort your spouse has handled. Thank them specifically and sincerely.

Personal Reflection

What contributions have I started to take for granted? How can I show that I notice?

A Prayer for Today

Thank You, God, for the everyday gifts in our life together. Help me recognize and appreciate them. Amen.

This challenge is an invitation to practice care, not a test of worth or a substitute for professional support. Never use a challenge to pressure, control, or endanger a spouse.
