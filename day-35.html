The 40 Day Journey · Day 35 of 40

Practice Affection with Consent

Scripture for reflection: 1 Corinthians 13:5

Today's Coaching

Affection should communicate care, not obligation. People differ in comfort, timing, and preferred expressions of closeness. Respectful affection invites connection while honoring consent and accepting a no without punishment or pressure.

Put love into action

Your practice today

Ask your spouse what forms of affection feel welcome and meaningful. Share your own preferences while respecting each other’s boundaries.

Personal Reflection

How can we make affection feel safe, welcome, and genuine for both of us?

A Prayer for Today

Lord, help us express love with tenderness, respect, and consideration for one another’s comfort and consent. Amen.

This challenge is an invitation to practice care, not a test of worth or a substitute for professional support. Never use a challenge to pressure, control, or endanger a spouse.
