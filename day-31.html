The 40 Day Journey · Day 31 of 40

Make Room for Joy

Scripture for reflection: Proverbs 17:22

Today's Coaching

Joy is not denial of hardship. Shared laughter, play, and pleasant moments can restore perspective and remind couples that their relationship is more than a list of responsibilities and problems.

Put love into action

Your practice today

Choose one simple activity you both enjoy, or invite your spouse to suggest something lighthearted to do together.

Personal Reflection

What brings us joy, and how can we make room for it in ordinary life?

A Prayer for Today

Lord, renew joy in our relationship and help us enjoy each other while caring for what needs attention. Amen.

This challenge is an invitation to practice care, not a test of worth or a substitute for professional support. Never use a challenge to pressure, control, or endanger a spouse.
