The 40 Day Journey · Day 14 of 40

Disagree with Respect

Scripture for reflection: Ephesians 4:29

Today's Coaching

Disagreement does not have to become disrespect. Couples can hold different views while refusing insults, threats, contempt, or intimidation. A pause can help both people return to a difficult topic more constructively.

Put love into action

Your practice today

Agree to pause a tense discussion if either person feels overwhelmed, then choose a time to revisit it calmly.

Personal Reflection

What boundaries help our disagreements stay respectful?

A Prayer for Today

Lord, help us pursue understanding without wounding one another. Amen.

This challenge is an invitation to practice care, not a test of worth or a substitute for professional support. Never use a challenge to pressure, control, or endanger a spouse.
