The 40 Day Journey

This independent coaching journey offers one daily practice at a time. Move at a thoughtful pace, return to a day when needed, and focus on the actions you can choose for yourself.

❀

Progress, not perfection.

Each day includes a Bible reference, original reflection, practical action, personal questions, and a prayer. The purpose is to encourage intentional love, mutual respect, and continued growth.
