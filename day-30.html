The 40 Day Journey · Day 30 of 40

Keep Your Commitments

Scripture for reflection: Matthew 5:37

Today's Coaching

Trust is strengthened when commitments are realistic and followed by action. If circumstances change, honest communication and a new agreement are more caring than silence or repeated excuses.

Put love into action

Your practice today

Choose one promise you can fulfill today. If it is no longer realistic, explain promptly and agree on a clear alternative.

Personal Reflection

Which small commitments help my spouse feel secure and considered?

A Prayer for Today

God, help me keep my word, communicate honestly, and make commitments with care. Amen.

This challenge is an invitation to practice care, not a test of worth or a substitute for professional support. Never use a challenge to pressure, control, or endanger a spouse.
