The 40 Day Journey · Day 26 of 40

Keep Commitments

Scripture for reflection: Matthew 5:37

Today's Coaching

Reliability is one way love becomes tangible. When plans change, honesty and timely communication protect trust better than silence.

Put love into action

Your practice today

Choose one commitment you can fulfill today. If you cannot, communicate and agree on a realistic next step.

Personal Reflection

Which commitments strengthen our sense of security?

A Prayer for Today

Lord, help my yes be trustworthy and my communication clear. Amen.

This challenge is an invitation to practice care, not a test of worth or a substitute for professional support. Never use a challenge to pressure, control, or endanger a spouse.
