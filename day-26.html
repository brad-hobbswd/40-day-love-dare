The 40 Day Journey · Day 26 of 40

Handle Stress as Partners

Scripture for reflection: Ecclesiastes 4:9–10

Today's Coaching

Stress can make partners turn against one another when the real problem is the pressure surrounding them. Naming the stress and deciding together what can change helps a couple approach the situation as a team.

Put love into action

Your practice today

Identify one current source of stress. Ask what support would help and choose one manageable action together.

Personal Reflection

When pressure rises, what helps us remember that we are partners rather than opponents?

A Prayer for Today

God, give us patience under pressure and help us face difficult seasons with compassion and teamwork. Amen.

This challenge is an invitation to practice care, not a test of worth or a substitute for professional support. Never use a challenge to pressure, control, or endanger a spouse.
