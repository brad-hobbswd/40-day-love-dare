The 40 Day Journey · Day 6 of 40

Choose Humility

Scripture for reflection: Philippians 2:3–4

Today's Coaching

Humility is not believing you have no value. It is being secure enough to consider another person's needs and admit when you have contributed to a problem. Growth begins when we stop protecting our pride at the expense of connection.

Put love into action

Your practice today

Identify one part of a recent disagreement where you could take responsibility. Acknowledge it without adding a justification.

Personal Reflection

What makes it hard for me to admit when I am wrong? What could humility repair?

A Prayer for Today

Jesus, help me lay down pride and take responsibility for my part with honesty. Amen.

This challenge is an invitation to practice care, not a test of worth or a substitute for professional support. Never use a challenge to pressure, control, or endanger a spouse.
